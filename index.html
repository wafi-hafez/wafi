Real product, real result · Massage oil and cream for Al-Kahraman, my natural products store

From a supplier’s two-line description to a sell-out

35 bottles of oil and 23 jars of cream, 58 units in total, sold out in store within three days of publishing this copy. Online ordering isn’t available in Syria, so the call to action had to move customers from the post to a conversation with a single comment.

Before: the supplier’s copy

Natural massage oil in two varieties: cinnamon and ginger, and almond and cypress.

Natural body massage for total muscle relaxation. Very important for cracked skin. Helps deeply moisturize and firm the skin.

After: my copy

Who said massage is only for knots and cramps?

Massage plays a big role in our lives, physically and mentally, because it helps with:

Relieving muscle tension and pain. It relaxes tight muscles and eases the cramps that come from strain or long hours of sitting, reducing neck and back aches.

Reducing stress and anxiety. It calms the body and releases the happiness and relaxation hormones: comfort for the muscles and for the mind.

Better sleep. When the muscles loosen and the body settles, massage helps you fall into a deep, restful sleep.

But massage can’t be done dry. It needs a dedicated oil that helps you relax and lets the hands glide over the skin.

At Al-Kahraman, we offer the colocynth cream and the massage oil, with special properties for a session worth talking about.

One dot from you is all it takes. Comment “.” below and the details will reach you. Shipping available.

Why it worked: the supplier described an oil. I opened by challenging a belief, showed the reader three moments they recognise, and made replying effortless. Note: written in Levantine Arabic and translated for this page, for the local Syrian market. For US, UK and Canadian brands I write wellness claims to fit FDA, FTC and ASA guidelines.

Unsolicited spec rewrite · Healthcare talent platform · Not client work

A category description turned into a loss the reader already fears

Before: the live homepage hero

The Missing Link to Lasting Talent

A smarter way to hire and keep clinical talent.

After: my rewrite

Your best candidates are committing to someone else’s hospital before they graduate.

Build relationships with future clinicians while they’re still in training, so they choose you on graduation day and stay long after.

Meet your future clinicians first

The problem: the original names a category. It could sit on any hiring platform, and it gives the reader nothing to feel. The fix: the new headline names what the reader is losing right now, the subhead explains how to stop losing it, and the button continues that thought instead of saying “Get started”.

Concept piece · Welcome email for a fictional focus app, Stillgrid · Not client work

A welcome email that sends the reader to a first session, not just a guide

Stillgrid is an invented app that mutes notifications and runs timed deep-work sessions.

Subject: Your focus didn’t disappear Preview: Here’s how to get it back, one session at a time.

Hey %name%,

If focusing has felt harder lately, nothing is wrong with you.

The world is louder than ever: notifications, tabs, messages, everything competing for your attention at once.

Focus didn’t disappear.
It just got crowded out.

You’ve already made the shift that matters: you chose to change your environment instead of blaming yourself. Stillgrid mutes the noise while your session runs, so deep work can feel natural again.

So don’t overthink the start.
One task. One session. No distractions.

Your Focus Guide walks you through your first session, step by step.

Open your Focus Guide

Talk soon,
Maya
Stillgrid

P.S. Hit reply and tell me what steals your focus most. I read every response.

What I fixed in my own first draft: the link used to sit mid-email behind “Click here”, so the email ended on advice instead of an action. It now closes on a button that names what the reader gets. “Create isolation” became what the app actually does, and the sign-off became a person, because replies are what a welcome email needs most.
